Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products 100% natural?
Yes, all our products are strictly 100% natural and organic. We never use artificial flavors, colors, or preservatives in any of our food items.
Do you export internationally?
Absolutely. We have a robust export network and currently supply our premium Ceylon products to hotels, restaurants, and distributors across the globe.
Can hotels request custom packaging?
Yes, we specialize in supplying hotel room amenities and offer custom packaging sizes suitable for hospitality needs. Please contact our sales team for bulk inquiries.
What certifications do you hold?
Our manufacturing facilities adhere to strict international standards and are ISO, GMP, and SGS certified, ensuring the highest level of food safety and quality.
How long does local delivery take?
For orders within Sri Lanka, we typically process and deliver within 2-4 business days. Bulk or custom orders may take slightly longer.
