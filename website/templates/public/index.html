Are your products 100% natural?
Yes, all our products are strictly 100% natural and organic. We never use artificial flavors, colors, or preservatives in any of our food items, ensuring you get only the purest ingredients.
Do you export internationally?
Absolutely. We have a robust export network and currently supply our premium Ceylon products to hotels, restaurants, and distributors across the globe.
Can hotels request custom packaging?
Yes, we specialize in supplying hotel room amenities and offer custom packaging sizes suitable for hospitality needs. Please contact our sales team for bulk inquiries and personalized quotes.
How long does local delivery take?
For orders within Sri Lanka, we typically process and deliver within 2-4 business days. Bulk or custom wholesale orders may take slightly longer depending on the order size.
What certifications do your products hold?
Our products are ISO 22000 and GMP certified, and independently tested by SGS. These certifications reflect our unwavering commitment to food safety, quality, and international standards.
Do you offer minimum order quantities for B2B?
Minimum order quantities vary by product and packaging type. For most B2B orders, our team works flexibly with you to match your specific needs — from small hotel pilots to large export contracts.
